# Ipi Tombe Circle — SEO and GEO launch playbook

## Positioning

**Search promise:** The place to discover Zimbabwean art, craft and design in Harare.

**Primary conversion line:** Made here. Made to matter.

**Supporting proof:** 18 Zimbabwean makers. One remarkable circle. Borrowdale Race Course, Harare.

## Keyword map

- Home: Zimbabwean art and craft Harare; local artists Harare; handmade Zimbabwe; Borrowdale craft market
- Maker pages: `[brand] Harare`; `[craft] Zimbabwe`; handmade `[product category]` Harare
- Future journal pages: Zimbabwean artists; Binga baskets; Zimbabwean ceramics; sustainable African art; handmade gifts Harare
- Visit content: things to do in Borrowdale; art and craft shops Harare; local shopping Harare; Zimbabwean gifts

Do not repeat keyword lists unnaturally. Give each future page one clear intent, a unique title and a useful answer.

## GEO / answer-engine recommendations

1. Publish one permanent, indexable page per maker with original biography, materials, process, price guidance and 3–8 captioned images.
2. Add concise factual answers on visible pages: what the Circle is, where it is, what visitors can buy, accessibility, parking, payment methods and verified opening hours.
3. Keep the same name, address, phone, hours and URL everywhere: website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect and social profiles.
4. Earn corroborating mentions from tourism, arts, press and maker websites. Ask each maker to link to their profile.
5. Publish source-worthy stories with named makers, dates, photography credits and first-hand detail. AI search rewards material it can quote and verify.
6. Structured data must match visible content. Never add ratings, prices, hours or claims that visitors cannot see and verify.
7. No special AI file is required for Google AI features. Crawlability, indexing, strong text, images and accurate entity data remain the foundation.

## Indexing checklist

1. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the final HTTPS domain in Vercel, then redeploy.
2. Verify the domain property in Google Search Console (DNS verification preferred).
3. Submit `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
4. Inspect the home page and 3–5 maker URLs; run live tests; request indexing.
5. Validate the home and maker URLs with Google Rich Results Test and Schema.org Validator.
6. Claim and fully complete Google Business Profile. Add the exact website URL, Borrowdale location, category, hours, phone, photos and updates.
7. Connect Search Console to Google Analytics; track `view_maker`, directions clicks, email clicks and update-list submissions.
8. Review Indexing, Core Web Vitals and search queries monthly. Improve pages with impressions but weak click-through rates.

## Social share assets

The site generates a 1200×630 product-photo composition at `/opengraph-image` and `/twitter-image`. This ratio is suited to Facebook, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, Slack and most messaging previews. Pinterest and Instagram posts are discovery content rather than link-preview formats; export campaign variants at 1000×1500 (Pinterest), 1080×1350 (feed) and 1080×1920 (Stories/Reels) using the same copy hierarchy and maker photography.
